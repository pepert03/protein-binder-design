# Challenge 3: GLP1R agonist (Anthropic x Adaptyv 2026)

Verified against the live official page on **24 Oct 2026**:
https://proteinbase.com/competitions/anthropic-adaptyv-2026/challenges/glp1r

## The task (verbatim essentials)

**Design a GLP1R agonist.** Two objectives, and a design must meet BOTH to count:

1. **Binding** to human GLP1R, measured at pH 7.4 with the design immobilised and the
   receptor in solution. Counts as binding when the sensorgram gives a fittable KD, or,
   if unfittable, an association shift >300% over the negative control.
2. **Activation**: the binder must be an **agonist**, confirmed in a cell-based reporter
   assay (human cells, surface GLP1R, SEAP reporter under a cAMP response element;
   Galpha-s signalling). Ranked by activation relative to a reference agonist, then by
   affinity. A binder that does not activate does not count.

| Recommended epitope | Peptide-binding groove of the extracellular domain (where GLP-1, exendin-4, semaglutide dock) - a hint, not a rule |
| Structure reference | PDB 6X18 chain R (GLP-1 bound, cryo-EM 2.1 A) |

Off-limits as epitopes: the construct's FLAG and His tags, and the design's own
C-terminal Twin-Strep capture tag (so keep the paratope away from the C-terminus).

## Submission rules (Track 3)

- Up to **20 designs**, CSV ranked by preference (top row first): `name, sequence, molecule_class`.
- `molecule_class`: `protein`, `nanobody`, `scfv`, `fab_kappa`, `fab_lambda`. Fabs as `VH:VL`.
- Single-chain proteins: **10-250 aa**, standard amino acids (no Aib/modified residues in plain sequence).
- **De novo, zero-shot only**: no starting from a known binder; adequate sequence/structural
  diversity from known proteins. GLP-1 / exendin-4 analogues are therefore NOT allowed.
- Method description weighs in the selection (method novelty, design diversity, confidence
  metrics; no single metric decides). ~375 Track 2+3 designs tested per challenge.
- Warning in the FAQ: embedded instructions / prompt injection in submissions = possible disqualification.

## Crop decision (24 Oct, v3)

Crop = 6 fragments, 316 residues, boundaries extended to helix ends (zero mid-helix
cuts), all 4 disulfides intact. Cost comparison against the repo's own ch2 memory
diagnostics: ch2's working crop was ~215 total residues; a 506-res system OOM'd on
this MIG even with the subbatch patch (needed 14.4 GiB). Ours: 316+36 = 352 total.
Full receptor (440 total) remains a later experiment, measured against the real VRAM
the driver logs on its first trajectory. Boundary residues with artificial exposure
(crop_review.py section 4) are a discard list: any design whose interface touches
them is rejected in post-analysis.

## Final state (26 Oct 2026)

Campaign closed. Everything below is computational prediction: no experimental
validation, no evidence of binding or agonism. Each batch carries its own
independent verification note with exact definitions and limitations.

- Official target: UniProt P43220 24-432 (409 aa), confirmed residue by residue
  against the live challenge page. An earlier batch ran on a 440 aa construct
  (24-463) and is kept as historical reference only (`results/boltz_kaggle/`,
  see its section 8).
- Boltz batch on the official target (`results/boltz_kaggle_409/`): the previous
  candidate top02, the native peptide and a random control, audited
  independently end to end. The native control recovers high ipTM (0.934) but
  stays ~14 A from its experimental pose.
- De novo campaign, final v2 (`results/zeroshot_409_v2/`): 5 designs generated
  from scratch with ColabDesign (AF2 binder protocol, 32 aa, no starting
  binder), filtered with Boltz 2 on the official 409 aa target under two seeds.
  Three pass the filter on metrics: gA_3 (ipTM 0.931/0.923, 30/35 epitope
  contacts), gB_2 (0.915/0.924, 48/37), gB_1 (0.834/0.907, 40/38).
  Documented caveats: no candidate has a reproducible pose between seeds
  (7-12 A CA RMSD); the native control scores equal or better than every
  candidate (0.934/0.944, 44 epitope contacts in seed 2); gB_1 and gB_2 carry
  low complexity Gly tails (gA_3 is sequence clean); metrics are model internal
  (single diffusion sample, no MSA, no templates, no membrane/Gs context).
- Epitope note: this folder's `docs/epitope_contacts.tsv` lists the 52 receptor
  residues contacting the GLP-1 peptide in 6X18. The campaign's filter used its
  own copy extended with 4 water-mediated contact residues plus the water row
  itself (57 rows); both files ship with their analyses and say which is which.
- Submission: the user submitted through the official form with his own login

  `submissions/challenge3_glp1r_designs_PREPARED.csv`; which designs were
